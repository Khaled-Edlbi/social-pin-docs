# Backlog

Generated by `node scripts/build-tree.mjs` from the task notes; do not edit, change the task's frontmatter. Every open task ordered by priority (Critical `critical` · High `high` · Medium `medium` · Low `low`, from `docs.config.json`), then the done ones. Status key: ⬜ To do · ❓ Needs a decision / info · ⛔ Blocked · 🔵 In progress · 🟡 Partial (done, `pending` notes left for a card or a person) · ✅ Done. A task is ⛔ when something in `depends` or `blockedBy` is not done. The deadline is the task's own or its feature's; the tree page colours it by how close it is. A bug also says how it ended in `resolution:` (Fixed `fixed` · By design `by-design` · Duplicate `duplicate` · Cannot reproduce `cannot-reproduce` · Won't fix `wont-fix`), which never replaces its status.

Columns as a board: [[Task Board]] (Kanban plugin format; also readable as a plain checklist).
