# ⚖️ D-06 · Yearly rename, old name freed, case-insensitive

**Answers:** [[ND-06 Pin name rules]] · **Decided by:** molham · **On:** 2026-10-25 · **Feature:** [[Gemma AI]], [[Profiles & Pin Name]]

> This card is the answer to [[ND-06 Pin name rules]]. The needs-decision card keeps the question, the facts and the options; this decision card says what was decided, why, and what it changes. `draft` while the answer is prepared; `decided` only by the PM or the owner, on the user's word, with `decidedBy` and `decidedAt`: then [[ND-06 Pin name rules]] shows Fulfilled by itself and the cards it blocked continue.

## What was decided

**Option A on ND-06: a yearly limit and private suggestions**, with two further rules.

- **Renaming is limited to once a year**, as s06 already says.
- **After a rename the old name is free** for anyone at once: it does not redirect to the new name and is not held back.
- **Suggestions** are offered when a name is taken, built **only from the person's own name**, never from other people's names.
- **Names ignore capitals**: `Omar.Fostok` and `omar.fostok` are the same name, so one person holds both.

Option B (no limit, the copy dropped from s06) is ruled out.

Answered on the product-questions page, 2026-10-22, recorded on molham's word on 2026-10-25.

## Why

- s06 already promises "once a year" and suggestions; option A keeps the design true.
- Building suggestions from the person's own name keeps the availability check from revealing who holds a taken name.
- Ignoring capitals stops look-alike names that only differ in case.
- Freeing the old name at once keeps names available. **Traded away:** a badge or card printed with the old name, or a link someone saved, can later lead to whoever takes that name next. Nothing redirects and nothing warns.

## What it changes

- [[Profiles & Pin Name]]: a rename within a year of the last one is refused; the old name is released at the rename; uniqueness and the availability check ignore capitals; suggestions are returned from the person's own name.
- [[Auth & Sign-up]]: claiming a Pin at sign-up (s06) uses the same rules and suggestions.
- [[Gemma AI]]: if suggestions are generated by Gemma, it gets only the person's own name as input.
- [[T-061 Decide Pin name rules]]: its decision is taken; the PM closes it.

## Still open

- Whether the first year starts at sign-up or at the first rename was not asked; the natural reading is that the first rename is free and the year counts from it.
- How a printed badge with a released name should behave is [[Badge & QR]]'s; if the badge QR encodes the name rather than a token, a released name sends the scan to someone else, which [[ND-12 What the badge QR encodes]] should weigh.
