No schema change, no new error code.

## How it works

- **Every visibility answer comes from the access engine** ([[Ring Access Model]]). Who shares with me, the Ring a requester shows, how many of my Rings someone can see and the mutual count are `RingAccessService` methods; a person's card is `RingProfileService.cardsForRings` rendering the Ring the engine picked, the same rendering as `/views` and Shared with me, so photos are signed links (D-16).
- **Addressing (D-10).** `POST /connections/requests { pinName }` resolves the Pin through the same check as `GET /views/pin/:pinName`: the account must be live and the requester must be able to open one of the person's Rings right now (`resolveDefaultAccess`). No such Pin, not shared, a block either way: the views' one 404 `E_RING_ACCESS_01`, body for body. So a stranger cannot connect by Pin name, exactly as they cannot open the card; the scan is the stranger's path (F-18). `{ againFor }` addresses the person of one of my own requests (a Sent row), and refuses a block or a gone account with the same 404. No user id is ever accepted.
- **By scanned code (D-10).** `{ qrToken }` on `POST /connections/requests`, `POST /blocks` and `GET /connections/relationship?qrToken=` is the token the card was opened with (the last segment of `https://socialpin.me/q/<token>`, D-12). `ConnectionRequestService.scannedPerson` resolves it through `BadgeService.resolvePresenter` ([[BD-18 Badge & QR Backend Delivery]]), which is the QR resolve's own decision (a genuine, live code; the presented Ring live; no block either way; membership not asked, D-28), and then requires the badge's owner to be an active account, as a Pin name does. So the scanner who could open the card can Connect or Block from it, without being in any of the person's Rings, and nothing else is asked. Every refusal (malformed, unknown, expired, reset or rotated token, an ended Ring, a block either way) is the views' one 404 `E_RING_ACCESS_01`, held to the resolve's 300 ms floor and counted against the scanner's limit (20 refusals or resolves a minute, then 429 `E_BADGE_01`); a success is not counted, so Connect and the button after a scan cost an event floor nothing. A request made this way records `source: SCAN` ("Scanned your badge" on the receiver's Incoming row). My own badge: Connect 400 `E_CONNECTION_REQUEST_04`, Block 404 `E_BLOCK_02`, relationship `NONE`. Exactly one of the addressing keys per call: two at once, or none, is a 400.
- **One pending request per pair (D-24).** Before inserting, the pair's lapsed requests are written `EXPIRED`; a connection answers 409 `E_CONNECTION_01`; their pending request answers 409 `E_CONNECTION_REQUEST_03`; my own pending request comes back with 200 (no duplicate). The partial unique index holds it under concurrency: the insert is not wrapped in a transaction, so when the index refuses it the state is read again and answered as above. Checked live: three taps at once give one request (201, 200, 200), two crossing taps give one request (201, 409).
- **Expiry (D-17).** `expiresAt` = sent + 30 days, exclusive. Every read treats a `PENDING` row past it as `EXPIRED`; the next request for the pair writes it. Incoming drops it at once; Sent keeps it.
- **Sent (D-13, D-27).** `PENDING`, `DECLINED` and `EXPIRED`; a declined one until 30 days after `respondedAt`, an expired one until 30 days after `expiresAt`. Accepted and cancelled are not returned. A block cancels the blocked person's pending request, so it disappears; it never reads `DECLINED`.
- **Which card a person shows on a Connections row** (`ConnectionService.cardsFor`): someone **who asked me** (incoming, or the request I accepted) shows their public card, their live default Ring's; someone **who shares with me** shows the card they share with me, from the Ring `resolvePeopleSharingWith` picks (since F-17: the oldest Ring I joined knowingly, else the one they put me in first, D-15), which is the Sent list's "as the card showed them"; anyone else shows only their Pin name. The base profile is never read beyond the Pin name. FR-14 asked for the requester's main-profile name: the default Ring's card follows the main profile unless that Ring set its own, and never shows what the requester switched off for everyone.
- **Accept (S5)** is one transaction: `RingMemberService.add` (the members route's own rules: administration at `MEMBER_MANAGER`, no block with the Ring's owner, never the owner), the connection upserted `ACTIVE` with `connectedAt` and `source`, and the request `ACCEPTED` with `grantedRingId`, written conditionally on `PENDING` so a cancel that lands in between rolls the whole accept back. The Ring must be `ACTIVE` and not ended (started or not); otherwise 404 `E_RING_02`. Someone already live in that Ring keeps their membership (no error). A block between the two people is 409 `E_RING_MEMBER_04`. The membership is placed (D-18): its Ring is never named to them. `grantedRingId` is written here and read by no route (D-14).
- **Contacts (S8, S12)** are my `ACTIVE` connections whose other side has an account (D-25), each with `ringCount` from the engine (live memberships in my live Rings, blocks excluded). `q` and `noAccess` filter the card I see and the count, so a search never matches a name the person has not shown me nor reaches a stranger; they run in memory over my own connections, then the page is cut. `GET /connections/count` uses the same `where`, so it always equals `meta.total`.
- **Detail (S9, D-23, D-29)** answers a contact, or a member of my Rings who is not one (`connectedAt: null`), with their non-removed memberships in my Rings oldest first and `mutualCount` (people `ACTIVE`-connected with both, not deleted, no block against either). Me, a stranger, a block either way and a deleted account are one 404 `E_CONNECTION_02`.
- **Disconnect (S11, D-15, D-26)** ends the connection and every membership of theirs in my Rings in one transaction, and sends nothing. My memberships in their Rings are theirs to manage and stay. A member who is not a connection cannot be disconnected (404).
- **Deleting an account (D-25)** ends, in `DELETE /users/me`'s own transaction (`UserService.softDelete` → `ConnectionService.endEverythingFor`): every `ACTIVE` connection it is on either side of (`REMOVED`, as a disconnect), every `PENDING` request it sent or received (`CANCELLED`, as a block; answered ones are left as they are), and every block it made or received (deleted). Nobody is told: the person leaves the other side's contacts, count, Sent, Incoming and Blocked people, and opening them answers the one "not available". Ring memberships are not touched here: the rest of a deletion is [[F-01 Account]]'s ([[BD-01 Account Backend Delivery]]).
- **Block (S13)** takes `{ qrToken }` (the scan's check, above), `{ pinName }` (the card's own check, so blocking from a card tells nothing a card would not) or `{ userId }` for someone I can already name by id (a contact, a member of my Rings, a requester in my inbox, someone I blocked before; anyone else 404 `E_BLOCK_02`, so the route probes nothing). In one transaction it keeps the person as I saw them, removes the memberships both ways, closes the connection (`BLOCKED`) and cancels pending requests both ways; 201 when made, 200 when it already existed (also under a double tap). The engine already honoured blocks; now they can be made. **Unblock restores nothing.**

### Gaps

- **`relationship` on the views**: FR-14 asked for it on `GET /views/pin/:pinName` and `GET /views/qr/:token`; it is its own route, `GET /connections/relationship?pinName=` or `?qrToken=`, because the views are [[F-12 View As & Pin Views]]' code. One more call when a card opens.
- **The 409 `E_CONNECTION_REQUEST_03` body**: the service puts `requestId` on it, but `AllExceptionsFilter` sends only `message` and `error` until [[S-87 Error Contract Story|Error Contract]] lands, so today the client reads the id from `GET /connections/relationship` (`THEY_ASKED`). No `ErrorCode` on the wire yet either.
- **A badge whose owner has since been deleted or suspended** answers the 404 after the token was found, so that one refusal is neither floored nor counted against the limit. The badge lookup and the Ring check do not read the owner's account status, so this check runs after them; the QR resolve itself (`GET /views/qr/:token`) does not check it at all, which is [[BD-18 Badge & QR Backend Delivery]]'s to decide.
- **Blocked people's photo**: `avatarUrl` is always null; only the name and Pin name are kept with the block.
- **A member who joined by code or invitation** shows only their Pin name on s47: memberships now record their origin ([[F-17 Ring Invitations & Join]]), but the card rule does not read it.
- **Where from**: `PIN_NAME` and `SCAN` are produced. `PINCAST` waits on PinCast, `EVENT` with `eventName` / `eventRingId` and the event filter on the incoming list on the Events features (S15, v1).
- **Rings I administer on s46**: accept already allows a Ring I administer at `MEMBER_MANAGER`, but no route lists them ([[S-31 Delegated Admins Story]]).
- Not part of F-14, asked by S-23 of other features: names and photos on the roster (FR-09), first-Ring order on `/views/pin` and the perspectives (FR-12, D-15), notifications (S-96), Gemma's suggested Ring (S-89), events in common (D-23).
- Contacts are filtered in memory over the caller's own connections: fine at MVP sizes, a limit to revisit for very large contact lists.
